Frequently asked questions

Is this passport photo maker actually free?

Yes. The editor, background removal, and the multi-copy 4x6 print layout are all free, with no signup, no watermark, and no paywalled export.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Cropping, background removal, and face alignment all run locally in your browser using in-browser AI models. Your photo itself is never sent to a server.

Which countries and documents are supported?

US, UK, Canada (passport and visa), India, China (passport/visa and travel document), Japan, Malaysia, Germany, Australia, Mexico TN visa, and Spain - each with its own exact size, DPI, and background requirements.

Will the background removal always be accepted?

Not necessarily. As of 2026, the U.S. State Department’s guidance says not to alter your photo with software, filters, or AI, including background replacement - only cropping, resizing, and similar format changes are safe for a US application. Rules differ by country, so check the official source linked on each country’s page before submitting.

What size and format do I get?

The exact millimeter/inch dimensions and DPI required for the document you pick, exported as a single photo and, where useful, a 4x6 print sheet with multiple copies.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs entirely in your browser on desktop or mobile - no app download, no account.

Photo requirements are set by each country’s government and can change. Always confirm the current specification on the official source linked from that country’s page before submitting.
